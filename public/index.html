Import your roster

Keep it updated automatically

Paste the link to your roster once: the link to the Leon roster PDF, or its calendar (iCal) subscription link. Each time you open the app, your flights are updated from it. The link works like a password for your roster: it is kept on the server and only used to read your flights.

Or import a PDF file once

In Leon, export your roster for the month as a PDF, then choose it here. Your flights appear only on your own calendar, and the file is read on this device without being uploaded.
